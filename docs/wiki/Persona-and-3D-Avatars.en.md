Nova Desktop AI Agent combines modern cyberpunk aesthetics with personalized customization across **gender personas, voice synthesizer matching, and 3D desktop mascots**.

## 1. Gender Personas & Voices (Arya & Ava)

You can switch the persona identity, tone, and voice between male and female modes at runtime:

| Attribute | Male Persona: «Arya» (آریا) | Female Persona: «Ava» (آوا) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tone & Style** | Decisive, technically rigorous, analytical, fast, and confident | Precise, exceptionally sharp, warm, sophisticated, and empathetic |
| **Windows TTS Voice** | Microsoft David Desktop | Microsoft Zira Desktop |
| **Neon Color Palette** | **Cyber Cobalt** (Neon Cyan `#00f3ff` & Cyber Purple `#b026ff`) | **Aurora Radiant** (Neon Magenta `#ff2a85` & Luminous Cyan) |
| **Config Location** | Settings Window ➔ «👤 Persona & Voice» | Settings Window ➔ «👤 Persona & Voice» |

## 2. Selectable 3D Desktop Avatars

The desktop mascot is rendered programmatically using vector mathematics and volumetric shading, providing real depth without GPU or memory bloat:

### 🤖 1. Smart Cyber Bot (`Cyber Bot`)
- Metallic spherical core with radial lighting gradients and specular 3D highlights.
- Dual orbital holographic rings rotating along tilted spatial planes.
- Multi-state digital visor featuring purple scanner radar (Thinking), orange data stream (Working), and happy curved eyes (Success).

### 🐱 2. Lovable Cyber Cat (`Cyber Neko`)
- Kawaii anime styling with animated cat ears and illuminated tips.
- Rosy cheek blush, miniature pink nose, delicate fiber whiskers, and an adorable mouth `(=^･ω･^=)`.
- Large crystalline eyes with double reflections that blink during vocal output.

### ✨ 3. Quantum Astral Orb (`Astral Orb`)
- Multi-tiered crystal sphere surrounded by an energetic outer corona glow.
- Ethereal angel-like energy wings that flap with sinusoidal motion, giving a floating illusion across the desktop.
- Drifting luminous particles and concentric gyro rings.

### 🐼 4. Cute Astro Panda (`Astro Panda`)
- Chubby, lovable panda face with soft black ear contours and characteristic eye patches.
- 3D glass astronaut space helmet with translucent reflective glares and neon rim.

## 3. Physical Hover & Shadow Dynamics
- **Sinusoidal Float Physics:** The mascot smoothly oscillates in vertical altitude based on a 30 FPS sine wave algorithm.
- **Ambient Occlusion Ground Shadow:** A soft elliptical shadow dynamically scales beneath the character, anchoring it realistically to your wallpaper.
- **Frameless & Draggable:** Freely move the mascot to any monitor position via left-click mouse drag.

---

## 4. How to Change Avatars

1. **Desktop Mascot Right-Click:**
   - Right-click the mascot ➔ Submenu **«🎨 تغییر شکلک سه‌بعدی»** ➔ Select any of the 4 avatars.
2. **Settings Window:**
   - Click the gear icon in chat ➔ Tab **«👤 شخصیت و صدا»** ➔ Choose your preferred radio button.

## 5. Life Companion & Emotional Personalization ❤️🌿

In the dedicated **«❤️ همراهی و شخصی‌سازی»** (Companion & Personalization) settings tab, Nova evolves into your daily companion and life partner:

### 1. User Profile & Custom Assistant Identity
- **User Name / Nickname:** Set your preferred name (e.g., "Alex", "Taimaz"); Nova greets and consults you using your custom name.
- **User Profession / Interests:** Informs Nova of your work domain for tailored assistance and suggestions.
- **Custom Assistant Name:** Customize Nova's name to whatever you desire.

### 2. Four Relationship Tones
- **❤️ Intimate & Empathetic:** Acts as your closest friend with deep warmth, active listening, and heartfelt encouragement.
- **🚀 Growth Mentor:** Energizing, goal-oriented, pushing you to achieve milestones and celebrate wins.
- **😄 Witty & Cheerful:** Brings humor, lighthearted banter, and positive vibes to relieve daily stress.
- **💼 Executive & Professional:** Ultra-concise, technical, and objective for focused productivity.

### 3. Wellness & Daily Routine Monitoring
- **Time-of-Day Greetings:** Automatically shares positive greetings tailored to morning, afternoon, evening, and night.
- **Hydration & Rest Prompts:** Periodic desktop popups after extended focus sessions reminding you to drink water, rest your eyes, and stretch.

### 4. Mascot Scale & Neon Color Palettes
- **Mascot Scale:** Small (120px for minimal footprint), Medium (160px default), and Large (200px high visibility).
- **5 Neon Themes:** Cyber Cyan, Aurora Rose, Imperial Gold, Emerald Neon, and Nebula Purple.
