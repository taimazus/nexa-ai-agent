## 1. Persona Ecosystem & Voice Synthesis Pairing

Users can align agent persona, gender, and interaction style to their workflow:
* **Gender & TTS Voice Pairing:**
  * Male personas (NexaMehr, SamYar, Artin, Parsa): paired with male SAPI voices (`Microsoft David`).
  * Female personas (NexaNoo, Nila, Tara): paired with female SAPI voices (`Microsoft Zira`).
* **Diverse Behavioral Tones:**
  * **Intimate & Empathetic (`intimate`):** Warm, supportive, caring companion.
  * **Technical & Executive (`technical`):** Rigorous, analytical, code and system focused.
  * **Witty & Energetic (`witty`):** Cheerful, humorous, uplifting workspace presence.
  * **Growth Mentor (`growth_mentor`):** Goal-oriented, accountability-focused, motivational.

## 2. Gallery of 8 Vector Desktop Avatars (Mascot Avatars)

All desktop mascots are rendered natively using vector math via PyQt6 `QPainter`, avoiding heavy 3D engine GPU overhead:

1. **🤖 Cyber Bot (`robot` / `cyber_bot`):**
   Metallic sphere with radial lighting gradients, orbital holographic rings, and dynamic visor animations (radar, equalizer, happy arcs).
2. **🐱 Cyber Neko (`cat`):**
   Kawaii anime-style cat with glowing neon ears, blush cheeks, delicate whiskers, and natural speech-synchronized blinking.
3. **✨ Astral Orb (`sphere`):**
   Multi-layered crystal orb with a radiant corona glow and ethereal wings oscillating in a gentle sine wave.
4. **🐼 Astro Panda (`panda`):**
   Chubby panda wearing a futuristic glass astronaut helmet with subtle specular reflections.
5. **🎧 DJ Headset (`dj_headset`):**

   Minimalist geometric wireframe orb suitable for executive and corporate environments.

## 3. Physical & Visual Mascot Dynamics
- **Floating Motion:** Subtle vertical sine-wave hovering simulating natural breathing.
- **Ambient Ground Shadow:** Dynamic elliptical shadow scaling with altitude for an authentic 3D hover sensation.
- **Draggable Everywhere:** Left-click and drag the mascot anywhere across multi-monitor setups.
- **Three Mascot Sizes:** Small (120px), Medium (160px standard), Large (200px full detail).
- **5 Neon Color Schemes:** `Cyber Cyan`, `Aurora Rose`, `Imperial Gold`, `Emerald Neon`, and `Nebula Purple`.

---

## 4. How to Customize and Switch Avatars
1. **Right-Click Mascot on Desktop:** Navigate to "🎨 Change Avatar" and select your preferred avatar.
2. **NexA Settings Window:** Configure defaults in the "Persona & Voice" or "Companion & Personalization" tabs.
3. **Voice/Text Commands:** Ask NexA directly: *"Change your avatar to DJ headset"* for autonomous switching.
